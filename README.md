## 🌟 Overview

Fusion Care is a two-stage AI/ML framework that integrates **Ayurvedic phenotyping (Prakriti)** with **modern clinical, cognitive, and lifestyle assessment** to support early Alzheimer's disease risk assessment.

The framework combines traditional medical knowledge with evidence-based clinical indicators to generate personalized risk prediction and health recommendations. The architecture is designed for future integration with wearable devices, EEG, neuroimaging and digital biomarkers.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🧠 Two-stage AI/ML pipeline
- 🌿 Stage 1: Ayurvedic Prakriti Classification
- 🩺 Stage 2: Alzheimer's Risk Prediction
- 🤖 Personalized Ayurveda + Clinical recommendations
- ⚡ FastAPI backend
- 📊 Explainable risk scoring
- 🔬 Future-ready architecture
